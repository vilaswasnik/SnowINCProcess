═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
                              ASSIGNMENT LOGIC CRITERIA
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

🎯 SMART ASSIGNMENT ALGORITHM:
   1. Current Load (less than 80%)
   2. Shift Schedule (AM/PM alignment)
   3. Recent assignments (fair distribution)
   4. Skill matching and domain expertise
   5. Availability status and response time

📊 CAPACITY INDICATORS:
   • 🟢 Available: 0-60% workload
   • 🟡 Busy: 61-80% workload  
   • 🔴 Overloaded: 81-100% workload

⚡ ESCALATION TRIGGERS:
   • No team members available (<80% capacity)
   • Critical priority incidents (P1)
   • Cross-team domain expertise needed
   • Manager approval required for resource allocation
